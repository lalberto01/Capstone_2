# Capstone_2
Python Capstone

## Project Description

This project will analyze sales data for EmporiUm, a bookstore chain that also sells tech gear, art supplies, and other products.

I will compare sales performance, customer activity, product category trends, and store performance. The goal is to make a recommendation for where the company should focus attention next quarter.

## Assigned Territories

- Region: South
- Sales Territory 1: Florida
- Sales Territory 2: Texas

## Project Questions

1. Who are the territory managers, store IDs, and cities for Florida and Texas?
2. What is the monthly total revenue for in-store sales in both territories?
3. Which stores are the top-performing stores?
4. Who are the top customers in each sales territory?
5. What are the monthly transactions and revenue by product category?
6. Where should marketing focus next quarter?
